This space sits at the intersection of music, words, and community. Through my piano blog, I document the ongoing adventure of growing the Global Piano Network—a mission to turn our cities into shared creative stages.

The Global Piano Network is building a world where music is accessible to everyone, everywhere. By mapping public and semi-public pianos and connecting people for spontaneous jam sessions, we transform urban spaces into vibrant hubs of connection. Our mission is to democratize music-making and foster a sense of belonging, creating a global cultural commons rooted in participation, creativity, and mutual support.
